# Suspicious URL Assessment

## Objective

Evaluate URLs for indicators of potentially unauthorized or suspicious activity.

## Assessment Criteria

### URL Structure

Review:

- Naming Patterns
- Length
- Use of Keywords
- Unusual Characters

### Hosting Information

Analyze:

- Hosting Provider
- Domain Reputation
- Infrastructure Consistency

### Content Review

Verify:

- Content Availability
- User Experience
- Technical Behavior

## Assessment Outcomes

- Low Risk
- Medium Risk
- High Risk

## Deliverables

- URL Assessment Report
- Risk Classification
- Supporting Evidence
