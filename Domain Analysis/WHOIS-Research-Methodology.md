# WHOIS Research Methodology

## Objective

Utilize publicly available registration data to support domain investigations and identify relevant ownership patterns.

## Research Process

### Information Collection

Collect:

* Domain Name
* Registrar
* Registration Date
* Expiration Date
* Name Servers

### Registration Review

Analyze:

* Domain Age
* Registration Patterns
* Registrar Reputation
* Renewal History

### Correlation

Identify potential relationships through:

* Shared Registrars
* Similar Registration Timelines
* Infrastructure Similarities

## Documentation

Record findings in a structured format for future reference and reporting.

## Deliverables

* WHOIS Investigation Summary
* Registration Timeline
* Supporting Evidence
